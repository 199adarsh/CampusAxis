# CampusAxis Onboarding

CampusAxis connects your university academic portal directly to ChatGPT.

## Available Capabilities
1. **Attendance Tracking**: Check overall aggregate attendance or deep-dive into specific subject lecture logs and non-credit condonations.
2. **CIE & Marks**: View Continuous Internal Evaluation test performance, scores, and grade standings.
3. **Curriculum Notes & Study Material**: Search and read syllabus-aligned unit notes, formulas, and references synced from Moodle.
4. **Academic Q&A Tutor**: Ask questions grounded in your course curriculum and lecture material.
5. **Hackathons & Competitions**: Discover upcoming collegiate and national hackathons with eligibility criteria.
6. **Faculty Tools**: Broadcast official announcements and evaluate student condonation/NC requests.
