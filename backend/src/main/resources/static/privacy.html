Privacy Policy for CampusAxis

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

CampusAxis ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CampusAxis academic portal and its associated Model Context Protocol (MCP) plugin for ChatGPT and Codex. This Privacy Policy strictly adheres to the OpenAI Plugin Guidelines, detailing what data we collect, how it is used, with whom it is shared, and how users maintain control over their data.

1. Categories of Personal Data Collected

We apply data minimization principles and gather only data strictly required to fulfill the specific academic request:

  • Student Identifier: Permanent Registration Number (PRN) and Date of Birth (DOB) solely during initial OAuth 2.1 authentication.
  • Academic Performance & Attendance: Course attendance records, aggregate percentages, lecture logs, and Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) test scores.
  • Course Material Queries: Subject names, unit topics, and search keywords for study notes and syllabi.
Prohibited & Restricted Data Policy: CampusAxis does NOT solicit, collect, or process Payment Card Information (PCI-DSS), Protected Health Information (PHI), government IDs (SSN/Aadhaar), GPS location coordinates, or full chat transcripts.

2. Purpose of Processing

Data collected is used exclusively for:

  • Delivering real-time attendance calculations and non-credit (NC) status checks.
  • Summarizing student CIE marks and performance evaluations.
  • Fetching syllabus-verified notes and campus announcements.
  • Matching relevant collegiate and national hackathons based on degree domain.

We do not use student data for surveillance, behavioral tracking, profiling, advertising, or model training.

3. Categories of Recipients

Data is never sold, leased, or monetized. Data is shared exclusively with:

  • Host University / Institution LMS & ERP: To retrieve authorized student academic logs.
  • OpenAI Platform (via MCP runtime): Responses to user-initiated queries are returned directly through the secure MCP protocol.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Timelines

  • Access Tokens: Digitally signed JWT tokens expire automatically within 24 hours.
  • Transient Responses: Query results (attendance figures, notes, CIE marks) are processed ephemerally and not persisted in permanent databases by the MCP server.
  • User Deletion Right: Disconnecting the CampusAxis connector in ChatGPT immediately invalidates all active session tokens.

5. User Controls & Choices

Users have full autonomy over their integration:

  • Revocation: You can disconnect or revoke the CampusAxis plugin at any time directly in ChatGPT Settings > Connectors.
  • Scope Control: Permissions are restricted to the explicitly authorized scopes (openid, student).

6. Security Measures

All communication requires HTTPS (TLS 1.3). Authorization follows OAuth 2.1 Code Grant with mandatory PKCE (RFC 7636). API keys and client secrets are never exposed to clients.
