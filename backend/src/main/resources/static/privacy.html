Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

CampusAxis ("we", "our", or "us") provides an academic intelligence and student management platform for universities. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using CampusAxis and its associated ChatGPT apps, plugins, and MCP endpoints.

1. Information We Access

When you authenticate with CampusAxis using your Student PRN and date of birth, we access academic records solely to provide authorized services:

  • Course attendance percentages and lecture logs.
  • Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) test scores and assignment grades.
  • Enrolled subject curriculum and syllabus notes.
  • Official campus announcements.

2. How We Use Information

Data retrieved from your institution is used exclusively to answer your queries in real time. We do not sell, rent, or monetize student personal data or academic records to third parties.

3. Authentication & Security

All communication between ChatGPT, your client, and CampusAxis uses encrypted HTTPS / TLS 1.3. User authentication is governed by OAuth 2.1 authorization code grant with PKCE (RFC 7636). Access tokens are short-lived, digitally signed JWTs that can be revoked at any time.

4. Data Retention

We do not store your private chat prompts or model outputs permanently. Cached academic session data is removed upon session termination or token expiry.
